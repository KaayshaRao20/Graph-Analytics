=== Part B: Task 7: Retrieve All Students Studying in Semester 5 ===

Query:
MATCH (s:Student)
WHERE s.semester = 5
RETURN s.studentId AS StudentID, s.name AS Name, s.semester AS Semester, s.email AS Email
ORDER BY s.studentId;
