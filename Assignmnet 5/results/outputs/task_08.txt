=== Part B: Task 8: Find All Courses with More Than 3 Credits ===

Query:
MATCH (c:Course)
WHERE c.credits > 3
RETURN c.courseCode AS CourseCode, c.courseTitle AS CourseTitle, c.credits AS Credits, c.level AS Level;
