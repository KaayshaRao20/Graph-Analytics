=== Part A: Task 5: Count the Number of Nodes Belonging to Each Label ===

Query:
MATCH (n)
RETURN labels(n)[0] AS Label, count(n) AS NodeCount
ORDER BY NodeCount DESC;

Results:
Label | NodeCount
------------------------------------------------------------
Student | 5
Course | 5
Faculty | 2
Department | 2
